Preserve user material separately from agent brainstorming. A working title or
brief written by Codex does not make its subject a user-approved article.
Record direct user ideas with their wording/source; flag uncertain origin.
Keep short posts and longread angles possible; do not select a first series,
assign authors, schedule, crosspost or publish unless requested.

Distinguish proposed, accepted and implemented. Verify licences, comparative
claims and current features before public copy; do not turn private source
material or unfinished features into public promises. New substantive reasoning
belongs in a dated discussion; update current summaries with source links.

This folder contains planning and internal article briefs, not a deployed CMS.
Engine/editor choice remains open. Do not copy experiments into public content
or treat economic scenarios as actual invoices or personal financial quotes.
