# Family income: Dutch tax, pension and insurance inputs

Date: 2026-10-10. Purpose: bounded inputs for an illustrative full-time family-business model, not a personal tax return or insurance quotation. Current legal form is one-owner eenmanszaak; no agreed partner remuneration. Do not divide its profit equally between spouses by assumption.

## 2026 tax inputs

For a person below AOW age, Box 1 bands are €0–€38,883 at 35.75%; the next €39,543 up to €78,426 at 37.56%; excess at 49.50%. The first band includes national insurance contributions: do not add a second AOW/Wlz charge on top. [Belastingdienst, Box 1 rates](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/hoeveel-inkomstenbelasting-betalen), [national insurance](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/sociale_verzekeringen/premies_volks_en_werknemersverzekeringen/volksverzekeringen/hoeveel_moet_u_betalen).

Entrepreneur Zvw is 4.85%, with maximum contribution income €79,409 in 2026. Maximum annual contribution per person is therefore €3,851.34 (calculated). It is separate from Box 1 and the insurer's nominal health premium. Employment in the same year affects the available maximum: the full-time future scenario must be separated from the current employment-plus-business year. [Belastingdienst 2026 Zvw](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/fisin/fisin2026/inkomensafhankelijke_bijdrage_zorgverzekeringswet).

MKB profit exemption is 12.7% of profit after entrepreneurial deductions; its tax benefit is limited to 37.56% in 2026. Thus applying the bands mechanically to 87.3% of profit understates tax for high incomes unless the deduction-rate adjustment is included. Eligibility as income-tax entrepreneur must be established. [Belastingdienst 2026 MKB](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/mkb-winstvrijstelling-2026).

Safe initial conservative scenario: one earner; ignore entrepreneurial deductions, tax credits, mortgage and other private deductions, and pension/AOV tax relief. Apply the three income-tax bands and Zvw maximum to profit after ordinary business operating expenses. Explicitly label omitted relief; this is an illustrative conservative budget, not an exact tax estimate or guaranteed upper bound for every personal circumstance. No assumed income splitting. A refined entrepreneur case can separately model the MKB benefit/cap.

## Pension contributions

Lijfrente contribution deductibility requires qualifying pension saving plus sufficient personal jaarruimte/reserveringsruimte. The 2026 annual allowance depends on 2025 circumstances, including pension already accrued in employment. Therefore household savings of €6k/€12k/€24k annually cannot automatically be deducted in full. The published 30% concerns pension-premium basis, not a universal deduction of 30% of turnover. [Belastingdienst deduction rules](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/werk_en_inkomen/lijfrente/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies/aftrekken-lijfrentepremies), [allowance calculator](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/aftrek-en-kortingen/content/hoe-bereken-ik-mijn-jaarruimte), [new rules](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/werk-en-inkomen/content/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioenen-wat-betekent-dat-voor-mij).

The three household pension amounts remain sensitivity assumptions, not an assessed adequate pension. Count them above €120,000 net household living income. A conservative model funds these out of after-tax cash until deductible room and ownership of contributions are verified.

## Insurance inputs

KVK reports AOV premiums often €100–€300 per month; another KVK overview gives an average €200–€300 gross/month. These are broad references, not quotes for either founder or evidence that this premium insures their desired income. Price depends on profession, age, insured benefit, waiting period and benefit duration. For a provisional two-person scenario, €500/month household (€250 each) is a transparent midpoint assumption; sensitivity €300–€800/month household can be used without presenting it as a verified market range. AOV replaces earnings after qualifying incapacity, not weak sales. [KVK AOV](https://www.kvk.nl/starten/alles-over-een-arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering/), [KVK consequences](https://www.kvk.nl/wetten-en-regels/hoe-je-gevolgen-van-arbeidsongeschiktheid-beperkt/).

Qualifying periodic-benefit AOV premiums can be deducted in private income tax as income-provision expenses, not charged as business operating costs; lump-sum policies differ. Initial cash model can conservatively omit relief, then refine after policy choice. [Belastingdienst entrepreneur AOV](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-ondernemers).

Basic health insurance is separate. Government publishes a 2026 average nominal premium €157/person/month (€3,768/year for two adults, calculated), plus possible excess/extra cover. First check whether existing €10k/month living budget already includes the family's current health premiums. Do not add them twice. If user explicitly intends all personal insurance above that budget, show them separately. [Rijksoverheid health premiums](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/zorgverzekering/premie-zorgverzekering).

Business liability, professional indemnity, cyber/legal and term-life coverage need a separate choice of risks and quotes. No sourced quote or accepted budget exists here; do not imply AOV covers all these risks. A generic contingency may be added as an explicit modelling assumption.

## Limits

Rates are 2026 inputs, not a forecast for the year the founders leave employment. Exact personal taxes need ages, employment/other income, deductions, pension accrual and agreed partner compensation. The single-owner conservative model is sufficient to understand the order of magnitude without requesting those details now.
