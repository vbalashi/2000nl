New substantial experiments get a named study folder and a short README:
question, owning issue, status/date, inputs and their rights, method/commands,
results/artifacts, limitations, next step. Keep generated outputs with their
study and follow existing ignore/security rules; never commit private datasets,
credentials or licensed material just to make evidence portable.

A result, a discussion decision and a deployed implementation are three different
states. Link between them instead of duplicating claims. Published articles live
in the media workflow, not in the experiment evidence folder.

Some active dictionary work/data may exist only in an owning issue worktree or
ignored local directory. Locate that work via its issue/worktree and verify its
entrypoint; a local dataset's existence is not a canonical completed study.
